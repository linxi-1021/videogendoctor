You are evaluating whether a generated video follows the given specification.

Input:
1. A video specification or ShotIR-style instruction describing the expected content.
2. Sampled keyframes from the generated video, each with a timestamp.

Your task:
Inspect the provided keyframes and identify any visible failures where the
video content deviates from the specification. For each failure you find:
- Name the failure type
- Describe the visual evidence you see in the keyframes
- Propose a repair suggestion

Only report failures that are visually supported by the frames.
Do not invent failures that are not visible.
